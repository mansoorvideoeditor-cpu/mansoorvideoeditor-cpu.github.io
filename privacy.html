Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

funnel-uploader is a private automation tool used by its owner to schedule and upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

Data access

The app uses Google APIs (YouTube Data API, Google Drive API, Google Sheets API) solely to upload videos, read video files, and read/write upload schedules. All data stays within the owner's own Google account. No data is shared with third parties.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the app owner via the YouTube channel associated with this tool.
